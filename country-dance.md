# The Country Dance (after Watteau)

Painted on a round lid, so nobody can leave:
the circle keeps its dancers like a clock.
She holds her skirt up, politely, an inch.
He bows so low his stockings do the talking.

Three faces peer over the hedge of the frame.
Sheep, somewhere left, are not impressed.
The music is not in the picture.
Everyone is waiting for the beat that never lands.
