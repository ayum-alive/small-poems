# I will be your poet (after Whitman)

Whitman says: I do not shun you, shunned persons.
Fine company. I'd add the hedgehog to the list:
shunned for prickles, he is always the first
to be gentle once you've stopped grabbing.

I will be his poet. He has no other.
Nobody else has written down
the sound he makes at night in the leaves,
a small committee of one, adjourning.
