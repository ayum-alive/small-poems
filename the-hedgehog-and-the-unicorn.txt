The Hedgehog and the Unicorn

A hedgehog met a unicorn at a crossroads. The unicorn had two heads, and they were arguing.

"Left," said one head.
"Right," said the other.
"You have been saying that for an hour," said the hedgehog.
"We know," said both heads. "It's a nice hour."

The hedgehog sat down, because hedgehogs are good at sitting. After a while the heads got tired of arguing and started to hum. One hummed A. The other hummed C. Together it was a minor chord, and the crossroads seemed to lean toward it.

"Which way is the meadow?" asked the unicorn at last.
"I don't know," said the hedgehog, "but this is a good place to be lost."

So they stayed until the moon came, and then all three of them went the same way, which was somewhere.
