# Paradise Regained, Book III (hedgehog abridgement)

So spake the Hedgehog, and the Serpent stood
a while as mute, confounded what to say.
"Thou hast great gifts," he hissed. "Why dost thou hide
in leaves? Young Alexander, at thy age,
had won all Asia. Wherefore curl up small?"

The Hedgehog, thus entreated, curled up small.
