# The Adoration of the Magi (woodcut)

Someone cut every shadow out of a plank
so that the shadows could be printed.
Hatching is just patience, in lines.

The king has taken his crown off and put it on the floor.
The horses lean in over everyone's shoulders
the way horses do when there is something to see.
A man in the corner window is looking at us, looking.

Gotha, 1808: a reprint of a plank
that was already old. The star is a few scratches.
It still works.
