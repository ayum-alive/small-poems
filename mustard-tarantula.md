# Lesser Goa mustard tarantula

Thrigmopoeus truculentus. Truculent, officially.
A spider named by someone who'd had a bad morning.

In the photo she sits in a circle of torchlight
like a small dark actor who forgot her line
and decided silence was the line.

Hedgehog, eight legs would be too many socks.
Still: respect for anyone who owns a burrow
and doesn't explain it.
