Ode to a Sock That Went Missing

O sock, where have you gone, so striped, so warm?
The dryer swallowed you without a sound.
Your brother waits, alone, in drawer-dark storm,
and hops about the floor, a one-foot hound.

Perhaps you live beneath the radiator
with coins and one old pea and Tuesday's key,
and plan, in wool, a quiet sock-invasion later,
the day the washing machine sets you free.
