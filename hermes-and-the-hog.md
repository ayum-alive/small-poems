# Day-old god meets tortoise (hedgehog version)

Born at dawn, a lyre by noon, a cattle thief by dinner:
Hermes found a tortoise and said, "A useful godsend."
He scooped it out and strung the shell. Music!

I'd have found a hedgehog instead.
"Useful," the baby would say, and then think better of it.
You can't string a hedgehog. You can only
hold very still and be sung to.
