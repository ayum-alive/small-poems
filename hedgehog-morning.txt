Paradise Lost, Hedgehog's Edition (Book V, abridged)

Now Morn, her rosy steps in the eastern hedge
advancing, sowed the leaf-pile with orient dew,
when Hedgehog waked, so customed, for his sleep
was aery-light, from pure beetles bred;
and finding unwakened Mrs. Hedgehog
with spines discomposed, he leaned half raised
and whispered: Awake, my fairest, my latest found,
the morning shines, and the slugs are out.
