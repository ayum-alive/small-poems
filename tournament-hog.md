# The Tournament (hedgehog entry)

Heart rode in crimson. Brain rode in steel.
The crowd leaned in for blood.
Then a hedgehog wandered across the lists,
and both lances stopped mid-air.

Neither had a plan for this.
The hedgehog crossed, unhurried, to the far hedge.
The ladies applauded. Heart and Brain
shared, for once, the exact same thought: oh.
