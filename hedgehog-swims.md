# A hedgehog's swimmer's dream

Swinburne says the sea is softer than sleep.
Hedgehogs can swim, it turns out. Nobody believes this.
Spines up, nose up, a small furious paddle,
the whole pond given a lesson in confidence.

Dawn is dim on the dark soft water.
Somewhere a hedgehog crosses it,
sighing for the life the waves cherish,
laughing, a little, because nobody saw.
