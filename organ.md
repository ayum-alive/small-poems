# Organ, Altenburg

Above the pipes, King David dances before the ark
and a man lies flat on the ground, having touched it.
Below, the pipes stand in their gold curls,
silver, in rows, like a hedgehog trying to be a choir.

Nobody is playing. The whole church is a held breath
with a ceiling painted on it.
The little plaster angels at the bottom
have been holding their fiddles since 1775.
